### Day/Night cycle with zombie jets
**What:** Implemented full 20-second day/night round cycle with zombie jet enemy type
**Why:** Andrew's feature request — adds wave variety and survival horror element
**Details:**
- 20-second round timer (1200 frames) counts down during PLAYING state
- Day wave ends → night phase begins with "NIGHT FALLS..." announcement (green glow)
- Night wave ends → dawn transition with "DAWN BREAKS..." (golden glow), advances to next wave
- Boss waves (every 5th) skip night mode — timer just resets
- Smooth nightBlend lerp (0→1 over ~60 frames) for gradual sky transition
- Night sky: dark navy gradient, stars visible with twinkling, moon with craters replacing sun, eerie green/purple atmospheric glow
- Clouds darken to grey silhouettes at night
- Zombie jet enemy type: dark green (#2a4a2a) damaged jets with torn/jagged wings, broken tail, pulsing green cockpit glow
- Zombie behavior: slow (1.5-2.5 speed) but relentless homing toward nearest player, wobble animation, no guns — ram damage only (25 dmg)
- Zombie spawning: continuous from top/left/right edges during night, rate ramps from every 40 frames to every 15 frames
- Green particle explosions on zombie death (spawnGreenExplosion), distinct zombie death sound
- Timer HUD: top center, white during day, green pulsing during night, red pulsing when <5 seconds
- Night atmosphere: green lightning flashes, blue/white player engine trails, brighter enemy bullet tracers
- Audio: eerie descending tone for night transition, rising chord for dawn, low bandpass + sine for zombie death
- Zombies die instantly on player ram collision, not clamped to X bounds (enter from sides)
- Day wave completion logic unchanged; night has its own timer-based end condition
